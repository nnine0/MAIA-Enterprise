MAIA (Multi-Adapter Inference Architecture) is an AI Governance Operating System. It solves the existential conflict between rapid AI innovation and the strict regulatory constraints of the financial industry. MAIA hard-codes regulatory principles directly into the AI's operational architecture. The system is thereby mathematically constrained to operate within a bank's legal risk appetite.
